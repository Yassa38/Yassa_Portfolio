MOEEN

Sign language recognition from raw video

MOEEN recognizes American Sign Language words straight from RGB video clips. An I3D network reads spatial and temporal motion together, so no pose or keypoint step sits in front of the model.

  • Modernized the WLASL I3D pipeline with i3d_modern, PyTorch automatic mixed precision and YAML configs for efficient training and checkpointing.
  • Trained with transfer learning on the WLASL dataset in Kaggle environments.
  • Built and deployed a Streamlit demo with video upload, dataset exploration and live webcam recording that returns Top-K confidence scores.

Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative

Heart disease prediction

How far can a dataset shrink before models suffer?

A classification study that compares several models, then uses PCA to cut the feature space and tests ensemble methods on what remains.

  • Prepared the data with preprocessing, feature scaling and a stratified train-test split for balanced classes.
  • Support Vector Machine gave the highest accuracy before dimensionality reduction.
  • AdaBoost and a Voting Classifier performed strongly after PCA, showing that ensembles hold up on reduced data.
